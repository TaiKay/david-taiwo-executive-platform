# Operational excellence is a leadership practice

Operational excellence is often treated as a technical concern. It is, more fundamentally, a leadership choice: the consistent decision to make the important work easier to see, understand and improve.

The strongest teams build systems that carry their standards forward. They document decisions, measure what matters and leave room for people closest to the work to shape its evolution.
